How SubhYatra Works

Your ride is just a few taps away

Getting a ride with SubhYatra is simple, safe, and affordable. Here's how it works.

Book Your Ride in 6 Easy Steps

1

Download & Sign Up

Download the SubYatra app from Google Play Store and create your account with your phone number or email.

2

Enter Your Destination

Enter where you want to go. Our app will show you the estimated fare and available vehicle options.

3

Choose Your Vehicle

Select from Bike, Car Basic, Car Premium, or SUV based on your needs, budget, and number of passengers.

4

Get Matched

We'll match you with a nearby verified driver. You'll see their name, photo, vehicle details, and rating.

5

Track Your Ride

Track your driver in real-time as they arrive. Share your trip with friends and family for added safety. 6

6

Pay & Rate

Pay with cash or eSewa at the end of your trip. Rate your driver to help maintain service quality.
